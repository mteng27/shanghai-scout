## Shanghai, with time to look around

Beyond the skyline, Shanghai has another story along the water: former factories, reused creative spaces, university buildings and everyday riverside parks. This edition brings those places together so visitors can choose a stretch that interests them and spend time there.

The guide follows Shanghai's waterway. A trip to Suzhou city is a separate outing.

## What makes the edition useful

| Feature | What visitors can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Eleven illustrated places** | Get a visual introduction to the river's parks, buildings and cultural stops. |
| **Interactive place selection** | Choose a landmark on the website and open its introduction and planning notes. |
| **Flexible outings** | Pick a short walk, an art-and-architecture half day, or selected stops across a full day. |
| **A sense of place** | Connect recognizable stops such as 1000 Trees, M50 and Waibaidu Bridge with the river around them. |

The illustration is a way to explore the places; use a live map for navigation. Choose a few stops rather than treating all eleven as a checklist.

## Who it is for

- First-time visitors adding a slower neighborhood outing to the classic sights.
- Return visitors looking for architecture, art and a different view of the city.
- Travelers who enjoy choosing a few places and leaving room to wander.
