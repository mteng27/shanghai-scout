# Shanghai Scout

**Discover Shanghai, at your own pace.**

An independent English-language travel guide to Shanghai, with illustrated city walks and practical tools for planning your visit.

**[Explore Shanghai Scout →](https://shanghaiscout.com/)** · [Travel guides](https://shanghaiscout.com/guides/) · [Planning tools](https://shanghaiscout.com/tools/) · [中文介绍](README.zh-CN.md)

## Find your Shanghai

A first visit, a short stay or another chance to explore: build a trip around the time you have and the places that interest you.

- **First visits:** connect the classic sights with a useful hotel base and a manageable itinerary.
- **Deeper exploring:** spend more time in neighborhoods, museums and local experiences.
- **Short stays:** choose a focused route and account for airport transfers and your return journey.

## Small tools for practical decisions

- **[First-Day Setup Checker](https://shanghaiscout.com/tools/first-day-setup-checker/):** review your phone, payments, maps, hotel address and airport transfer preparations.
- **[Hotel Area Selector](https://shanghaiscout.com/tools/hotel-area-selector/):** compare a Shanghai base with your budget, trip length and travel style in mind.
- **[Museum Reservation Checker](https://shanghaiscout.com/tools/museum-reservation-checker/):** understand which museum visits need more advance planning.

The website is free to use and does not require an account. The reservation checker provides planning guidance; bookings are handled through the relevant venue's channels.

## About the project

Shanghai Scout is maintained in Shanghai for international visitors. It combines practical travel preparation with time for neighborhoods, walks and cultural experiences. The three-day itinerary is a starting point you can adapt to your visit.

This repository contains the project's public introduction. Explore the guides and use the tools on [shanghaiscout.com](https://shanghaiscout.com/).
